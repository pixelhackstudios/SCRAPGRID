**Welcome, adventurer, to SCRAPGRID.**

SCRAPGRID is a persistent, text-based MMORPG set in a sprawling off-world frontier built from rust, poorly solidered salvage, forgotten infrastructure, and alien technology that refuses to die.

Here, civilization survives by improvisation. Settlements grow around crashed machinery and abandoned industrial systems. Ancient power grids hum beneath scrap-built cities. Corporate enclaves tower above crowded districts of mechanics, smugglers, mercenaries, hackers, scavengers, traders, and people simply trying to make it through another day.

The world is vast, layered, and shared. Roads cross deserts and industrial wastes. Cities rise vertically through stacked districts and elevated transit systems. Mines, tunnels, buried facilities, forgotten colonies, and stranger things extend far beneath the surface. Other players inhabit that world alongside you—traveling, fighting, trading, exploring, building reputations, and leaving their own marks on SCRAPGRID.

There is no single path through it.

Go somewhere you shouldn't. Take a job. Join a crew. Make an enemy. Get lost underground. Find something nobody was supposed to find. Sell it to the wrong person.

Welcome to SCRAPGRID. Try not to break anything important unless you can do so without getting caught.
